The Heritage of Mechanical Chronometry

Founded on the foundational premise that true horology is an uncompromising mechanical science, VintageGearFord restores, calibrates, and conserves the world's finest vintage timepieces.

Manhattan Atelier

The Mercer Street Horology Vault

Situated at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, the VintageGearFord workshop features temperature-stabilized laminar airflow chambers, antique Swiss staking tools, and specialized acoustic timing machines.

Our watchmakers reject superficial battery-powered quartz shortcuts. Every movement entrusted to our care is fully disassembled down to its individual screws, pinions, and balance hairsprings, undergoing rigorous microscopic inspection and manual re-regulation.

Endangered Discipline

Preserving Classical Hand-Finishing

While mass-market luxury brands rely on automated CNC milling and robotic oiling dispensers, our watchmakers preserve centuries-old hand-finishing traditions.

From hand-rolled anglâge along bridge perimeters to specular black polishing on steel chronograph levers, we honor the tactile soul of high watchmaking. Every finished movement that leaves our benches represents an imperishable monument of human mechanical genius.

Guild Declarations

Our Four Horological Commitments

1. 100% Mechanical

Zero quartz batteries or printed circuit boards; we celebrate the pure physics of gears, springs, and levers.

2. Provenance Integrity

All historical patina, factory chamfers, and vintage serial engravings are safeguarded against destructive over-finishing.

3. Five-Position Timing

Every movement is adjusted for isochronism across five spatial positions and three temperature gradients.

4. Lifetime Archival Support

We maintain custom tooling and micro-lathes to fabricate bespoke replacement components for centuries to come.
