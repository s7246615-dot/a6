Horological Disclaimer

Review the formal institutional commitments, client privileges, and operational protocols governing VintageGearFord.

1. Section 1 Provisions

The horological treatises, gear train calculations, and watch care recommendations published across VintageGearFord are presented solely for educational enrichment and historical interest. While our workshop conducts empirical timing tests and acoustic evaluations, movement longevity involves diverse environmental factors, magnetic exposure, and variable maintenance routines. Patrons rely upon our watch care guides, timing estimates, and regulation protocols entirely at their independent personal discretion.

2. Section 2 Provisions

Intensive mechanical watchmaking, balance staff poising, and vintage calibre restoration work demand appropriate physical pacing, optical ergonomic habits, calibrated manual force, and supportive workshop routines. Horological treatises published on this website do not constitute certified vocational certification or commercial warranty coverage. VintageGearFord disclaims legal liability for timing performance variance, balance damage, or personal injury sustained while attempting referenced benchwork methodologies.

3. Section 3 Provisions

Digital representations, photographic imagery, and color swatches presented across our online catalog are calibrated to reflect actual products as faithfully as possible. However, because antique metal patinas and vintage dials reflect ambient lighting uniquely, minor discrepancies in shade and texture will inevitably occur. Ambient lighting conditions and monitor screen calibrations influence perceived tones. Patrons are encouraged to inspect timepieces at our Manhattan studio.

4. Section 4 Provisions

This website may contain hypertext links directing visitors to third-party horological societies, museum archives, or independent toolmakers not operated by VintageGearFord. Such external references are provided solely as informational resources, and our organization does not endorse or assume responsibility for external web content. Visitors accessing third-party internet locations do so under the distinct terms and privacy protocols established by those independent operators.

5. Section 5 Provisions

For formal clarification regarding any workshop documentation, material provenance disclosures, or horological accreditation standards, please submit written communication directly to our administrative office. You may direct inquiries to VintageGearFord Atelier, 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, or contact our patron advisory desk directly by telephone at +1-888-777-5845. We remain dedicated to providing transparent, verifiable craft information to all collectors and watch connoisseurs.
